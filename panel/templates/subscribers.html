What each list shows

Online
Has a live session now.
Stale
Session open but no update from the router for 15 minutes (power cut or cable pulled).
Offline
Active customers with no live session.
Expired
Package end date has passed.
Expired Online
Expired but still connected. They are disconnected at the next login.
Profile Disabled Online
Suspended by you but still connected.
Internet Disabled
Suspended, or expired with no grace period. They cannot log in.
Expiring ...
Active customers whose package ends within that time.
MAC Binded
Login is locked to one router or device MAC.
Grace Period On
Allowed to stay online for a few extra days after expiry.
Recent Auto Renew
Renewed or paid in the last 7 days.
Balance
Unpaid extra service fees (see Extra Service Fees).
