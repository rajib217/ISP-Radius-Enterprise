Connect Telegram

  1. On your phone open Telegram, search @BotFather, send /newbot, choose a name. It gives you a token.
  2. Paste the token below and press Save.
  3. Open your new bot in Telegram, press Start and send it any word.
  4. Press Find my chat ID, then Send test message. For a group: add the bot to the group, send a message there, then press Find.
The server needs internet access to api.telegram.org. The token is stored in the panel database; anyone with the token can send messages as your bot, so keep it private.
