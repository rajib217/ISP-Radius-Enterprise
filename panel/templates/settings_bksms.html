The customer sends money to your bKash number and writes their subscriber ID (the ID column of the subscriber list, also shown in their portal) in Reference; their username or mobile number works too. An SMS-forwarder app on the phone that gets bKash's SMS sends it here. The package is renewed when the money plus the customer's balance covers the price; otherwise the money goes into their balance. Each TrxID is used only once.

The sender name the phone shows for bKash's SMS; several separated by commas. Leave empty only if your app cannot send the sender.

{{ hook }}
Keep this address secret, like a password: whoever has it can send payments in. If it leaks, make a new address.
  1. Install an SMS-forwarder app on the phone that holds the bKash number, and let it run in the background (turn off battery saving for it).
  2. Add a rule: sender bKash, forward to the address above, method POST.
  3. Body: JSON such as {"from": "%from%", "text": "%text%"} (use your app's own placeholders). A form, or the SMS alone as the body, also works.
  4. Send yourself a small test payment with a subscriber ID in Reference and watch it appear below.

Instead of a phone: a SIM800C (or any AT-command USB GSM modem) with the bKash number's SIM, plugged into this server's USB port. The panel reads its SMS every 5 seconds, books the bKash payments by the same rules as above, and deletes them from the SIM. The switch Renew customers from forwarded bKash SMS above must be on too.
