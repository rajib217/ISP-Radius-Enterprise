When on, the scheduler pushes RADIUS session starts and stops every minute, changed subscribers and NAS routers every 5 minutes, and re-sends every subscriber at 02:15 and the whole NAS list at 02:20 each night. Before turning it on, every router must exist on the NAS page with the IP it sends syslog from: the NAS push replaces the engine's router list, and the first push refuses to drop a router the engine is still receiving logs from.
